# The Hex — Ship Bible

## Identity
- **Name:** Hex
- **Class:** Freighter / Gunship hybrid (disguised as freighter)
- **Crew capacity:** 8 people
- **Callsign:** HEX (hand-stenciled in green, starboard side)

## Philosophy
> "A fortress disguised as a freighter — built not to win, but to endure."

## Owner / Builder
- **Dox** — built the Hex himself, poured grief into the hull
- Every system has a backup, visibly bolted on after the fact
- The over-engineering is intentional — paranoia made physical

## Hull & Exterior
- Wide-body, low, thick, aggressive silhouette
- Weathered bone/tan plating — heavy battle damage, burn marks, grime streaks
- Cockpit: offset to port, sunken, small windows (protected/paranoid)
- Amber running lights along hull edges
- Blue emissive thruster (rear) — only cool color on the ship
- **Markings:**
  - "SECTOR 7X" — red stencil, port side (the X feels added later)
  - "HEX" — bright green, starboard (hand-stenciled, not factory)
  - Registry numbers: 712, 773, 111, 711

## Special Detail — Moon of Ash Alloy
- Built into the **engine core** from Moon of Ash alloy
- Slightly darker, slightly iridescent texture — visibly different from rest of hull
- The emotional heart of the ship — Dox's grief made material

## Interior Layout (8-person crew)
- **Cockpit:** 2 seats (Dox + co-pilot), offset port
- **Crew quarters:** 4-6 bunks, cramped but reinforced bulkheads
- **Engineering bay:** Where the Moon of Ash alloy lives
- **Cargo hold:** The cover story is always "freight"
- Interior walls: double-plated, redundant piping everywhere

## 3D Build Notes
- Source: blocky freighter base mesh (Sketchfab/BlenderKit)
- Hull texture: weathered dirty metal plate, tileable
- Decals: "SECTOR 7X" red stencil, "HEX" green irregular stencil
- Engine housing: darker iridescent material (Moon of Ash)
- Lighting: amber point lights (hull edges), blue emissive (thruster)
- Export target: GLB for interactive 360° web viewer
